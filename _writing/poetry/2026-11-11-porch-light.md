The bulb is the cheap one,\
the kind that hums a little.\
I leave it on when I walk the dog,\
and it is still on when I come back.

Nothing dramatic lands in that yellow.\
A moth. The neighbor's tire.\
Me, counting the steps I already know.

The dark does the rest of the work.\
The light is only there so I can find the door.
