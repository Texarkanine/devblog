The first cup was for the work. It went cold beside the keyboard, the way it always does, and I drank it anyway because it was there.

The second cup was not for the work. The house was quiet in the particular way a house gets after midnight, when the refrigerator has the only opinion left. I filled the kettle because my hands wanted a job that would finish.

I stood at the counter and did not open the laptop. The mug was the heavy one, the one with the chip on the rim that I keep saying I will throw out. It fit the hand I was using. That seemed like enough of a reason.

Outside, the porch light was on. I had forgotten it again. From the kitchen it was only a small square of yellow, and it made the rest of the yard look finished. I let it burn. Somebody might still be coming up the walk, even if the somebody was just me in the morning.

The second cup tasted like the first. I drank it slower, which was the whole difference, and when it was gone I rinsed the mug and turned the kitchen light off and left the other one on.
