title: Kitchen Radio
description: A song for the radio that stays on after the kitchen is done.
author: texarkanine
tags:

---

The dishes are finished and the radio is not.\
It talks to the spoons in a voice from another town.\
I dry the same plate twice so the song can end.

Turn it down, the night says, people are sleeping.\
I turn it down.\
I do not turn it off.

Somebody far away is singing about a kitchen too.\
Theirs has a window.\
Mine has the light above the sink, and this.

When the song is over I stand there anyway,\
hand on the dial, as if the next one might know my name.
