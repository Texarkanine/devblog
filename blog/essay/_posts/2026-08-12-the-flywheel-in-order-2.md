This is the outline of the essay. Not the essay. Revised after the first prose pass: the Times fit is the reveal, not an early contrast. Attempted solutions get their own chart set. The Reddit money line lives there, not on the break.

**Thesis (do not dump this in the lede):** Every platform in this fight is the same hub with three loops. AI breaks different sides. The responses already on the table either draw a circle after the consumers left, feed the entity while starving the asset, filter producers while still wanting the whole market, or shrink into a coherent corner. After that corner looks like the only honest move, one other shape closes: staffed editorial producers, paid in something the new consumer will actually pay, selling verified-human output to AI labs. Money is the only such something found so far. That shape does not transfer to social platforms, because their product *is* public demand telemetry. There is no template for the rest of us, including the author.

The title may wink. The body should not spoil the reveal before Codeberg has been called Amish.

The discarded first draft opened on Netflix. That spine is still dead. Structure is now five movements.

## Act IV - Reveal: the Times still has a customer

After Amish looks like the end of the road: the Times is probably doomed on the current track, *and* it has a way to thrive that no other specimen has found.

Do not explain this by previewing Reddit's failure before Act III. Act III already showed Reddit's check. Now contrast: the new consumer is the lab. Producers must eat something that consumer will pay. Money is the only such something found so far. Times writers eat money. Reddit posters eat reputation. Same rent being charged; the denomination the producers spend does not match.

Social/editorial split lives here: Times never needed pull telemetry; choosing *is* the product. Reddit/SO *are* the telemetry. Patronage cannot replace a compass.

Anthropic / Project Panama receipt. Exclusivity windows, commissions, patronage. Click-through optional once paid.

Optional fourth-ish chart: thick `AI ==>|Money| NYT`, writers still paid in money, readers stay on the aggregator. The point of the similar charts paying off.

**Verdict: mechanical hit for staffed editorial media only.** Market risk remains. Not a template.

Do not crown Codeberg. Do not crown the Times as advice for Reddit.
